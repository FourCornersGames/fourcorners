Website Privacy and Cookies

fourcornersgames.ca · Last updated November 9, 2026

In short: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and it doesn’t load anything from other companies, so there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to accept or refuse. There are no forms; if you email us, we use your message only to answer you. Our games have their own privacy policies (links at the bottom of every page).

This page covers the website fourcornersgames.ca. What each game collects is explained in that game’s own privacy policy: YarnToss, Whiskers Cafe and Pocket Quest.

Who we are

This website is run by Four Corners Games (Four Corners G.P., Quatre Coins S.E.N.C. in French, a general partnership registered in Quebec), based in Quebec, Canada. Our Privacy Officer is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. You can reach our Privacy Officer at support@fourcornersgames.ca.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies. It also has no analytics or statistics tools, no advertising or tracking pixels, no social media buttons, and no embedded videos, maps or other content from another site. Our fonts and images are stored on our own site, so viewing a page doesn’t contact any other company.

Because nothing is stored on your device to follow you, we don’t show a cookie banner. If we ever add cookies or similar tools that aren’t strictly needed for the site to work, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., in the United States). As with any website, your browser sends the host technical information such as your IP address and browser type so that it can deliver the page. GitHub states that it logs the IP address of visitors to GitHub Pages sites for security purposes. We don’t receive these logs. GitHub handles them under its own privacy statement, and they may be processed outside Quebec and outside Canada.

If you email us

The website has no forms. If you write to support@fourcornersgames.ca, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to put in your message. We use it only to answer you and to deal with your request (for example a bug report, a purchase problem or a privacy request), and we keep it only as long as we need it for that. Please don’t send us passwords or payment details. Our email provider stores messages for us.

Links to other sites

Some links take you to other sites, such as our Discord server, Google Play or Google’s help pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may use cookies; this page doesn’t cover them.

Team-only pages

Some pages under /dev/ are tools for our own team and are not meant for the public. If a team member signs in there, the browser keeps the sign-in in session storage for that tab only (it is not a cookie, and it is deleted when the tab is closed or the person signs out), and the page connects to Google Firebase. Nothing is stored or sent if you don’t sign in.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it. Write to support@fourcornersgames.ca. We will reply within 30 days. If we cannot resolve a concern, you can contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date above.
