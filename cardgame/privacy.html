Privacy Policy

Card Game (working name) · Last updated November 22, 2026

In short: Card Game is always online. You sign in with Google Play Games (Android) or Apple Game Center (iOS) to play. We get a player ID from them. We do not get your email address or your real name, and we never store either one in our own data. Your profile name is built from word lists, so you never type a name. You add friends only with a friend code. There is no chat or messaging of any kind. By default, nobody can send you a friend request and you appear offline. The game has no ads, no analytics and no purchases. You can delete your account at any time. We never sell your data.

This policy explains what Card Game does with information, and what you can do about it. We are a small studio in Quebec. We tried to keep this page short and plain. “Card Game” is a working name: the final name of the game is not chosen yet. This policy covers the game while it is in testing and before its release. The game is for Canada and the United States.

Who we are

Card Game is made by Four Corners Games (Four Corners G.P., Quatre Coins S.E.N.C. in French, a general partnership registered in Quebec), based in Quebec, Canada. Our Privacy Officer is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. You can reach our Privacy Officer at support@fourcornersgames.ca.

Who can play

The game is for people 14 and older. The first time you open the game, it asks a neutral age question. If you are under 14, you cannot play. We do not keep your answer or your birth date. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 14. If you believe a child under 14 has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

What we collect

The game needs a network connection to load. To play, you sign in with Google Play Games on Android, or Apple Game Center on iOS. We use Google Firebase Authentication with that player ID. We collect:

  • your Google Play Games player ID or your Game Center player ID. We do not receive your email address, your real name or your password;
  • a profile name built from word lists, and an avatar you pick from a fixed set. You do not type a name;
  • your friend code. It is random, and you can make a new one at any time;
  • a one-time deletion code, which lets you delete your account by email if you no longer have the game;
  • your friend list, the friend requests you sent and received, and the players you blocked;
  • your cards, decks and game progress, saved on our servers;
  • your privacy settings;
  • your online status (Online, In a match or Offline), with a time stamp that the game updates while it is open. We keep only the latest value. We do not keep a history;
  • a list of the last 10 players you played against, visible only to you;
  • the reports you send about other players (reason, date and match), and the reports other players send about you;
  • the date you agreed and the version of this policy you agreed to.

Android and iOS accounts are separate. They cannot be joined, so friends and progress do not move from one system to the other.

We never store players’ email addresses or real names in our own data. We do not ask for them. Our code does not read them.

There is no chat or message of any kind between players. Players cannot type any text to each other. We cannot see any messages, because there are none.

Deck codes are text that you copy and share yourself. A deck code holds only card IDs. We do not receive deck codes.

Technical data. Like any online service, Google Firebase, which we use to run accounts, receives technical information such as your IP address and device type when your device connects.

Ads, analytics and purchases. The game has no ads, no analytics and no purchases. Before we add any of them, we will update this policy.

Getting the game. You install the game through Google Play or, later, the Apple App Store. They handle the download, updates and crash reports under their own privacy policies.

Why we collect it

  • to run your account and show your profile to your friends;
  • to let you add friends with a code, accept, decline, remove and block;
  • to show your online status to the friends you allow;
  • to apply your privacy choices;
  • to keep the game fair and safe: to read reports, deal with cheating and quitting, and limit abuse;
  • to answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or to build a profile of you.

Your privacy settings

These settings start at the most private choice. You turn on only what you want.

  • Friend requests: off. Nobody can send you a request until you turn them on.
  • Online status: “Appear offline” is on. You appear offline until you choose to share your status. You can turn sharing off again at any time.
  • Signing in and saving: you need to sign in, and we need to save your account, profile and friends data on our servers, to play. The optional settings above (receive friend requests, show online status) stay off until you turn them on. You can turn them off at any time.
  • Blocking: a player you block cannot send you a request, see your status, or be matched with you.

What other players can see

Other players can see your profile name, your avatar and, if you allow it, your online status. Your friends appear in your friend list. Players you faced recently can see your profile name during and just after a match. Other players cannot see your friend list. Nobody can search for you by name. They need your friend code. Only Four Corners Games can see and edit account data, through the Firebase console and a private admin tool, and only to give support and to moderate.

Reports and moderation

You can report a player for a bad name, cheating or quitting matches. A report holds your account ID, the other player’s account ID, the reason, the date and the match. A person at our studio reads each report. We can warn, restrict or remove an account after a person has reviewed a report. We do not ban accounts by automatic rules. We do not tell the reported player who sent the report.

Who we share it with

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and Cloud Functions) to run accounts, friends and online play. Google acts as our service provider. Your profile, friends and settings, including your online status, are stored in Google’s data centre region in Montreal, Canada (northamerica-northeast1). Firebase Authentication runs only in Google data centres in the United States. Your sign-in data (your Firebase ID, your Play Games or Game Center player ID and your IP address) is processed there, where the privacy rules are different from Quebec's. Google Play Games and Apple Game Center are run by Google and Apple, under their own privacy policies. Both companies are based in the United States. We assessed the privacy risks before sending data outside Quebec. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account exists. These are the other periods:

  • Recent opponents: 30 days.
  • Pending friend requests: 30 days, then they expire.
  • Reports: 12 months, even after the reported account is deleted. A report holds only internal account IDs, not profile names.
  • Inactive accounts: no sign-in for 12 months, then we delete them.
  • Online status: we do not keep it. We keep only the latest value while you are online.
  • Deleted accounts: we delete the data from our database within 30 days. Google, our service provider, then removes it from its own systems and backups within 180 days. We do not keep our own backups.

When you delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete Account), we delete your profile, friend code, friend list, requests, blocks, settings, recent opponents and your login link. Your friends no longer see you. Reports you made about others are deleted. Reports made about you stay for up to 12 months, to deal with abuse. They contain no email address and no real name. If you no longer have the game, you can ask us by email. Step by step: Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, give you a copy in a common format, or delete it. You can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to support@fourcornersgames.ca. We will reply within 30 days. You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from the game. If we cannot resolve a concern, you can contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

Security

We rely on Google’s security for stored data and limit what we collect to what the game needs. Our database rules deny access by default. No system is perfectly secure. We keep a register of privacy incidents. If an incident is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Commission d’accès à l’information as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Before we add ads, analytics, purchases, chat or anything else that uses more personal information, we will publish the updated policy here and tell you in the game.
