Delete your Card Game account

Card Game (working name) by Four Corners Games (Four Corners G.P.) · Last updated November 22, 2026

In short: you can delete your Card Game account yourself, any time, from inside the game. If you no longer have the game, email us and we will delete it for you.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open Card Game and tap Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm with Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS).

The game removes your account straight away. Your friends stop seeing you. We erase the remaining data within 30 days.

Or ask us by email

If you no longer have the game, write to support@fourcornersgames.ca with the subject “Delete my Card Game account”. Give us your friend code and your profile name. This is how it works:

  1. We mark the account “pending deletion” for 14 days.
  2. If the owner signs in during those 14 days, the game asks “Keep your account?” The owner can cancel the deletion.
  3. After 14 days, we delete the account.
  4. We reply by email when the request is closed. To protect players, the reply does not say if an account matched. The whole process ends within 30 days.

We delete the email thread when the request is closed. We never store emails in our own data.

What is deleted

  • Your profile name and avatar.
  • Your friend code, friend list, friend requests and blocks.
  • Your privacy settings and your consent record.
  • Your recent opponents list.
  • The reports you made about other players.
  • Your login link to Google Play Games or Game Center.
  • Your saved game progress.

What is kept, and for how long

Reports that other players made about you stay for up to 12 months, so we can deal with abuse. This is also true after the reported account is deleted. They contain no email address and no real name. We never store emails or real names in our own data.

Uninstalling the game does not delete your account. Accounts with no sign-in for 12 months are deleted.

Delete some data but keep your account

You can clear your recent opponents and friend list in the game. You can also ask us to delete part of your information without closing your account. Write to support@fourcornersgames.ca, give your friend code and tell us what you want removed. We will do it and confirm within 30 days.

For everything else about your information, see our Privacy Policy.
