1. Who can play

You must be at least 14 years old.

2. Fair play

We built the game to be fair to you. Difficulty comes only from the level number, or from your score in Endless mode. It does not change because of your device, your country, your purchases or how you play. The game never deals pieces in order to make you lose. Every level can be completed without boosters and without buying anything.

In return, play fair. Do not use modified versions of the game, and do not tamper with the game or its data, for example to change your coins or to get purchases without paying. We may not be able to help you if you do.

3. Ads

The test version shows panels marked “Ad placeholder”. The game draws them itself and no real ad is loaded. A later version may show real ads, such as a banner at the edge of the screen or an ad between levels. No ad interrupts a level in progress. Before a version with real ads is released, we will update our Privacy Policy.

Some ads are optional. You choose them, for example with a Watch Ad button, to get a reward. The reward is stated before you choose the ad. You never have to watch an optional ad to keep playing. Remove Ads removes the banner ads and the ads between levels. Optional reward ads stay, because you choose them.

4. Coins and purchases

Coins are a play currency. You earn them by playing, you can get them from optional ads, and you can buy them. You spend them on boosters (Undo, Hint, Loaf and Second Chance) and on box themes. Coins have no cash value. They cannot be transferred, sold or exchanged for money, including coins you bought.

Your coins are saved on your device only. They are not tied to an account or to Google Play. If you uninstall the game, clear its data in your Android settings, or choose Settings → Reset Progress, your coins are erased. Google Play cannot give them back, and coin packs cannot be restored. Box themes you bought with coins are also saved on your device only.

In the Google Play version of the game, the Shop sells these items with real money. The Shop opens only when you choose to open it.

  • Remove Ads, one-time purchase, CA$3.99: removes banner ads and ads between levels. Optional reward ads stay.
  • 250 Coins, CA$1.49, 700 Coins, CA$3.99, and 1600 Coins, CA$7.99: coin packs, which you can buy as often as you like. The coins are added to your game and used up like the coins you earn.
  • Starter Pack, CA$4.99, one per player: Remove Ads, 500 coins and the Cafe Cream box theme.

These are the prices in Canada at the time of writing. Google Play shows the price in your currency, with the taxes it shows, before you pay, and that is the price that applies. Google Play processes the payment under its own terms. Your purchase is delivered once Google Play confirms the payment. If a payment is pending, it arrives when it clears.

Remove Ads and the Starter Pack are tied to your Google Play account. You can restore them with Restore Purchases in the Shop, and they are restored if you reinstall the game. Restoring the Starter Pack gives back Remove Ads and the Cafe Cream theme, but not the 500 coins, because coins are not restored.

Purchases are optional. No purchase is needed to finish any level. If you are under the age of majority where you live, ask a parent or guardian before buying. We may change prices or the items on offer; this does not affect what you already have.
