Privacy Policy

If I Fits · Last updated November 14, 2026

In short: If I Fits has no accounts, no ads, no purchases and no analytics, and this version cannot connect to the internet. Your progress stays on your device. We do not collect, receive or sell any personal information through the game.

This policy explains what If I Fits does with information, and what you can do about it. We are a small studio in Quebec and we have tried to keep it short and plain. If I Fits is in testing; this policy covers the test version. If a later version adds ads, purchases or anything else that uses personal information, we will update this page before that version is released.

Who we are

If I Fits is made by Four Corners Games (Four Corners G.P., Quatre Coins S.E.N.C. in French, a general partnership registered in Quebec), based in Quebec, Canada. Our Privacy Officer is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. You can reach our Privacy Officer at support@fourcornersgames.ca.

Who can play

If I Fits is for people aged 14 and over. It is not directed at children. The game does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone.

What we collect

Through the game: nothing. The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or any other information about you. It does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. It does not read your advertising ID, your location, your contacts or your files.

On your device, the game keeps one save file so you can continue where you stopped. It holds only game information: the level you reached, your stars for each level, your best score, your coins, the box themes you own and the one you use, your language, your sound and vibration settings, and whether you have seen the How to Play screen. We cannot see this file. It is not included in Android’s cloud backup.

“Ad placeholder” panels. The test version shows panels marked “Ad placeholder” where ads may appear in a later version. They are drawn by the game itself. No ad is loaded, no advertising service is contacted and nothing about you is used.

Getting the game. You install If I Fits through Google Play. Google handles the download, updates and any crash reports under its own privacy policy, and gives us overall figures (such as how many installs and crashes there were) that do not identify you.

If you join a test. To give you access to a closed test, we add the email address of your Google account, or a Google Group you belong to, to our tester list in Google Play Console. We use it only to give you access to the test. If you send us feedback by email, we receive your email address and what you write.

Why we collect it

  • to let invited testers install the test version;
  • to answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or to build a profile of you.

How difficulty works

The game sets its difficulty from the level number, or from your score in Endless mode, and nothing else. Every player gets the same level for the same level number. The game does not use your device, your country or how you play to change the difficulty, and it never deals pieces in order to make you lose.

Who we share it with

The game shares nothing, because it collects nothing. Tester email addresses are held in Google Play Console; Google acts as our service provider and may process them outside Quebec and outside Canada, in jurisdictions with different privacy rules. Email you send us is handled by our email provider. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

The save file stays on your device until you choose Settings → Reset Progress, clear the app’s data in your Android settings, or uninstall the game. Each of these deletes it. We remove your email address from the tester list when the test ends or when you ask us to, whichever comes first. We keep emails you send us only as long as we need them to answer you and to improve the game.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, give you a copy, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to support@fourcornersgames.ca. We will reply within 30 days. If we cannot resolve a concern, you can contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

Security

We limit what we collect to what is needed: for the game itself, that is nothing. No system is perfectly secure. If an incident is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Commission d’accès à l’information as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Before a version of the game that uses personal information is released (for example one with ads or purchases), we will publish the updated policy here and tell you in the game.
