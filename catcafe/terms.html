Your purchase is delivered once Google Play confirms the payment; if a payment is pending, it arrives when it clears. If you are under the age of majority where you live, ask a parent or guardian before buying.

The Bakery Club gives its bonuses for the time shown when you buy it (for example 30 days). Buying it again adds that time to what you have left. If it is offered as a subscription that renews by itself, the store page says so before you pay, Google Play renews it until you cancel, and you can cancel at any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions); you keep the bonuses until the end of the period you paid for. The Club’s bonuses make the game faster and more generous; no recipe or bakery is only available to Club members.

Purchases are final, except as provided by Google Play’s refund policies and by your rights under consumer protection law, including Quebec’s Consumer Protection Act. You can ask for a refund through Google Play, or write to us. If a purchase goes through but it doesn’t arrive, write to support@fourcornersgames.ca with the order ID from your Google Play receipt and we will sort it out.

We may change prices or the packs on offer; this does not affect what you already have. Deleting your account removes your bakery, your items and your currency, including what you bought and any Bakery Club time left, without compensation. A subscription you bought through Google Play is not cancelled by deleting your account: cancel it in Google Play.
