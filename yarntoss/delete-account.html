Delete your YarnToss account

YarnToss by Four Corners Games (Four Corners G.P.) · Last updated November 7, 2026

In short: you can delete your YarnToss account yourself, any time, from inside the game. If you no longer have the game, email us and we will delete it for you.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open YarnToss and tap Settings (the gear on the main menu).
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm with your password, or with Google Play Games if that is how you sign in.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Or ask us by email

If you can’t open the game any more, write to support@fourcornersgames.ca with the subject “Delete my YarnToss account”. Send it from the email address you signed up with, or tell us your YarnToss username if you sign in with Google Play Games, so we can find your account. We will delete it and confirm within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: username, game progress, best score, in-game currency, skins, achievements and any runs in progress.
  • Your entries on the leaderboards, including every day of Challenge Mode.
  • Your reserved username, so someone else can use it.
  • Your login: your email address and password, or your link to Google Play Games.
  • The progress saved on the device you delete from.

What is kept, and for how long

Two small records, so we can deal with abuse such as offensive names:

  • the record of any username changes (your internal account ID, the old and new usernames, and the date);
  • a deletion marker (your internal account ID and the time you deleted the account), which stops a deleted account from being recreated to get around a leaderboard removal or a username cooldown.

Neither contains your email address. We keep them only as long as they are needed for moderation and to prevent abuse.

If you deleted your account before November 7, 2026, entries from earlier Challenge Mode days may still exist. Write to us and we will remove them.

If you only played as a guest, there is no account to delete: your progress lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Delete some data but keep your account

You can also ask us to delete part of your information without closing your account, for example to take your entry off the leaderboards or to erase your saved progress. Write to support@fourcornersgames.ca from your sign-up email address (or with your YarnToss username if you use Google Play Games) and tell us what you want removed. We will do it and confirm within 30 days.

For everything else about your information, see our Privacy Policy.
