Pocket Quest

A fantasy adventure that keeps going while you’re away

Your hero hunts, loots and levels up on their own. Open Loot Bags for new gear, choose a Path, fill your Spellbook and fight your way through forests, swamps, mines and dragon lairs. Then come back to everything they found while you were gone.

How it plays

Your hero fights on their own. You make the choices.

1

Hunt and loot

  • Battles play out by themselves, wave after wave, with a boss at the end of every hunt.
  • Open Loot Bags for new gear: wear it or sell it on the spot.
  • Away Loot is waiting for you when you come back.
2

Build your hero

  • Choose a Path and grow into its awakened form.
  • Fill your Spellbook with Spells that cast themselves.
  • Research, Relics, Familiars, Mounts and more make your hero stronger over time.
3

Adventure together

  • Everyone on a server starts close to the same day.
  • Chat with the server and send private messages.
  • Families, rankings and the Arena are on the way.

Choose your Path

Three ways to fight, each with its own Spells and its own look.

Every hunt ends with a boss. Some of them breathe fire.

Pocket Quest is in development and coming to Google Play for testing first. Ads are only ever a button you choose to tap, for a reward it names first.
