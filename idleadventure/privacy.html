Privacy Policy

Pocket Quest · Last updated November 7, 2026

In short: Pocket Quest saves your hero online with an account, and the game needs an internet connection to play. On Android, the game first offers to sign in with Google Play Games, so a returning player gets their heroes back; if you say no, you play with an anonymous guest account. We never ask for your real name or email. Your hero gets a random in-game name that you can change, and other players on your server can see it, your level, your Rankings numbers and what you write in chat. If you choose a picture for your hero, we review it before other players can see it. Chat messages are stored and may be checked by us to keep the game safe. Anything you buy will be paid through Google Play: we never see your card or payment details. The Android version will have optional ads from Google AdMob that you choose to watch for a reward (see Ads). We never sell your data, and you can delete your account any time from your portrait (top left) → Account.

This policy explains what personal information Pocket Quest collects, why, and what you can do about it. We are a small studio in Quebec and we have tried to keep it short and plain.

Pocket Quest is still in development and being tested. Purchases and ads are not in the game yet; this policy already describes how they will work, so it covers them from the day they arrive.

Who we are

Pocket Quest is made by Four Corners Games (Four Corners G.P., Quatre Coins S.E.N.C. in French, a general partnership registered in Quebec), based in Quebec, Canada. Our Privacy Officer is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. You can reach our Privacy Officer at support@fourcornersgames.ca.

Who can play

Pocket Quest is for people aged 14 and over. It is not made for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 14. If you believe a child under 14 has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

What we collect

When you play, the game needs an internet connection. On Android, if there is no account on your device, the game first offers to sign in with Google Play Games, so that a returning player gets their heroes back. If you say no, or on a device without it, the game creates a random anonymous guest account with Google Firebase the first time it opens. Either way, you don’t give a name or an email. The game is played on servers (worlds), and you have one hero on each server you play on. Each hero is saved with your account, so it can be restored on another device or after a reinstall. What is saved:

  • your hero’s progress: level, gear, Spells, Path, currencies, the hunts and dungeons you have cleared, and the rest of the game’s systems;
  • your hero’s name (see below) and its level, so the server list can show your heroes;
  • what you have bought, once purchases are in the game;
  • when the save was made, a save counter, the game version, and a random ID for each install of the game, so the game can tell your devices apart when it compares saves.

The game uploads your save about once a minute after a change, and when you close it or switch apps. It also keeps up to five older copies of each hero’s save (at most one an hour) in case something goes wrong. You can’t restore one from the game: if you need one, email us with your hero’s name and server and we will restore it for you. Only your account can read your saves.

Server time. To keep timers, daily resets and each server’s day fair for everyone, the game reads the current time from Google’s servers when it connects, instead of trusting your device’s clock. Nothing extra is collected for this.

Your hero’s name. Every hero starts with a random name such as AdventurerK7QM. You can change it from your portrait (top left) → Account. Names are unique on each server and are checked against a word filter. We keep the name with your account, together with how many times you have changed it.

Your picture (avatar). You can choose a picture from your device (Account → Avatar → Choose Picture). The game crops it to a square and shrinks it to 128 x 128 pixels, then sends it to us for review. We approve or deny it, and the answer arrives as a letter in your in-game Mail. Only an approved picture is shown, to other players on the servers you play on, next to your chat messages. The picture is stored with your account in Google Firebase (Realtime Database). You can remove it at any time (Account → Avatar → Remove), and it is deleted with your account. Don’t upload pictures of yourself or anyone else, or anything you don’t have the right to share.

Chat and private messages. On each server there is a World chat that every player on that server can read, a System channel for our announcements, and private messages between two players. When you send a message, we store its text, your hero’s name, your account ID and the time. Messages are filtered for offensive words when you send them. We also store your list of blocked players, a record of when you last sent a message (to limit spam), and the list of players you have private conversations with. Please don’t share personal information in chat.

Rankings. Each server has Rankings of every hero’s best-ever Power and furthest hunt. We store these numbers with your hero’s name so the Rankings can show them.

Letters from us. We can send you letters that appear in your in-game Mail, for example the answer about a picture. They are stored with your account until the game delivers them.

Reports and moderation. If you report a player or a message, we receive your report: your account ID, the player you reported, their name, the reason, the message text if there is one, where it was, and when. Only Four Corners Games can read reports. We read reports and may read the chat on a server to deal with them. We can delete messages, and we can ban an account that breaks the rules; a ban is recorded against the account ID.

If you play on Android with Google Play Games, you can sign in with it or link it to your account so your heroes come back on another phone. We receive your Google Play Games player ID, and Firebase keeps the public profile details that come with it (such as your Play Games display name) with your login. The game may offer, once, to use your Play Games display name as your hero’s name, and only if your hero still has its random default name. It is only used if you tap “Use It”; then it is your hero’s name, and other players see it like any hero name. Otherwise the game doesn’t show your Play Games name to other players; they only see your hero’s name. We do not receive your email address or your Google password.

Like any online service, Google Firebase, which we use to run accounts, saves and chat, receives technical information such as your IP address and device type when your device connects to it.

On your device, the game keeps a save file for each server you play on, your settings (sound, notifications), which server you last played, and a login token that keeps you signed in. Reminders the game shows, for example when a timer is ready, are scheduled on your device; nothing about them is sent to us, and you can turn them off in Settings or in your phone’s settings. We do not use cookies or analytics tools; the ads described under Ads will be the only third-party service that uses your advertising ID.

If you buy something (once purchases are in the Google Play version of the game), the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card number, bank details or any other payment information. Google Play gives the game a purchase record: the order ID, what you bought, when, and a purchase token. The game uses it to deliver your purchase, and keeps the token on your device until Google Play confirms the purchase is complete, so that no purchase is delivered twice. What you bought is then saved with your progress. Google Play also tells the game which country your Google Play account bills in, so the shop can follow that country’s rules (for example on paid items with random contents); the country is not saved.

Why we collect it

  • to run your account and save your heroes online, and to restore an older copy if you ask us to;
  • to restore your heroes on another device or after a reinstall, and to ask you which save to keep if two devices disagree;
  • to run the servers, keep timers fair and keep hero names unique;
  • to run chat and private messages, and to keep them safe: filtering, blocking, reports, removing messages, checking pictures before other players see them, and banning players who break the rules;
  • to show the Rankings of each server;
  • to send you letters in your in-game Mail, for example the answer about a picture;
  • to deliver what you buy and to help with questions about a purchase;
  • to show the optional ads you choose to watch and give you their rewards;
  • to answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information.

What other players can see

Players on the same server can see your hero’s name and level, the messages you post in World chat, with your name, and your hero’s best-ever Power and furthest hunt in that server’s Rankings. If you have an approved picture, players on the servers you play on see it next to your chat messages. If you used your Play Games name as your hero’s name, they see it like any hero name. A private message can be read by you and the player you sent it to (and by us, if it is reported or we need to check for abuse). Choose a name you are comfortable sharing, and don’t use your real name. Your saves, currencies and purchases are private to your account. The Arena, Families and Friends are planned: when they arrive, other players will also see what they show, such as your hero’s Path and look, and we will update this policy.

Who we share it with

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and the Realtime Database) to store your account, your saves, your hero’s name and the chat. Google acts as our service provider. This data is stored in Google’s data centres in the United States, and sign-in data handled by Firebase Authentication and Google Play Games may also be processed by Google elsewhere, outside Quebec and outside Canada, in jurisdictions with different privacy rules. Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google’s own terms and privacy policy; Google shares the order information described above with us and handles refunds. In the Android version, ads will be provided by Google AdMob, which receives the information described under Ads and uses it under Google’s own privacy policy, including outside Quebec and outside Canada. The game’s pages link to our Discord community; if you follow the link, Discord’s own privacy policy applies. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example to deal with illegal content in chat.

Ads

Ads are not in the game yet. When they arrive, the Android version of Pocket Quest will have optional rewarded ads only: an ad only plays when you tap a button that says what you get for watching it (for example a free Scroll read or time off a timer). There will be no banner ads and no ads that interrupt the game.

Ads will be provided by Google AdMob. So that ads can be loaded and ready when you ask for one, Google’s ad software will start when the game opens. It collects, and shares with Google:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • how you interact with ads (for example whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped);
  • technical information such as device type, operating system, and crash and performance data from the ad software.

Google uses this to show ads, measure how they perform, and prevent fraud. We will only ask for ads suitable for a general audience. Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google’s consent form will appear the first time you open the game, and you will be able to change your choice at any time from Settings. Elsewhere, ads may be personalized using your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We will not receive your advertising ID or your ad data ourselves; Google only gives us overall reports (such as how many ads were shown). Pocket Quest does not use analytics tools.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, your heroes’ saves and their older copies for as long as your account exists. A hero’s name stays reserved for as long as the hero exists. Messages you post in World chat and your private messages are kept until we remove them or you delete your account; we may also remove old messages from time to time. Your picture, whether approved or waiting for review, is kept with your account until you remove it or delete your account. Letters from us are kept with your account until the game delivers them. Reports and bans are kept for as long as we need them to keep the game safe, including after the account is deleted, so a banned player can’t simply start over; they contain account IDs, names and the reported text, not your email.

When you delete your account (your portrait (top left) → Account → Delete Account → Delete Forever), we delete right away every hero’s save and its older copies, your hero names and chat profiles, your Rankings entries, the messages you posted in World chat, your private conversations and inbox, your picture, any letters from us waiting in the server mailbox, your block list, then your login and its Google Play Games link. The game then closes, your progress is wiped from your device, and opening the game again starts fresh. Information collected by Google AdMob is kept by Google under its own policies. Google Play also keeps a record of each purchase (order ID, item, price, date and country), which we can see in our Google Play Console for accounting, tax and refund purposes, for as long as Google retains it. Step by step, including how to ask us by email: Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, give you a copy, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to support@fourcornersgames.ca. We will reply within 30 days. You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from the game. If we cannot resolve a concern, you can contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

Security

We rely on Google’s security for stored data, our database rules only let your account read and change your own saves and messages, and we limit what we collect to what the game needs. No system is perfectly secure. If an incident is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Commission d’accès à l’information as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the game before they take effect.
