1. Who can play

You must be at least 14 years old.

2. Your account, servers and heroes

The game makes an account for you the first time you play, so your heroes are saved online. On Android you can also link Google Play Games, which keeps your heroes when you change phones. A guest account that isn’t linked is lost if you lose your device or uninstall the game. Keep your Google account secure; you are responsible for what happens under your account. You can delete your account at any time from your portrait (top left) → Account.

The game runs on servers. You can have one hero on each server. Each server has its own day count, chat and Rankings. We may open new servers, and we may merge servers that are quiet; when two servers merge, rankings may reset and a name that is taken may get changed.

If two devices have both made progress on the same hero, the game asks which save to keep; the other one is replaced. We keep a few older copies of each save; if something goes wrong, email us and we will try to restore one for you. We can’t merge two saves or promise to bring back progress you chose not to keep.

3. Names and chat

Choose a hero name and write messages that are fine for everyone to see. Do not:

  • harass, threaten, bully or impersonate anyone, including us;
  • post hateful, sexual, violent or illegal content, or anything that targets a person for who they are;
  • share anyone’s personal information, including your own;
  • spam, advertise, sell accounts or items, or post links to scams or cheats.

You can block any player and report a player or a message from their player card. We review reports and may remove messages, change a name, mute or ban an account, or reset progress, depending on how serious it is. You are responsible for what you write; we don’t check every message before it appears, and we are not responsible for what other players write.

4. Fair play

Do not cheat, tamper with the game, its save data or your device’s clock, or use exploits, bots or automated tools to gain rewards or rank. Do not use more than one account to gain an advantage on a server. We may reset progress obtained unfairly, remove a player from rankings, or suspend accounts for cheating.

Gold, Gems, Crowns, Scrolls and other in-game items are part of the game. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred, sold or exchanged for money, including currency you bought. Items you open at random show their odds in the game before you open them.

Purchases are not in the game yet. When they arrive, in the Google Play version of the game you will be able to buy Crowns, packs and passes with real money. The price, in your currency, and what you get are shown before you pay, and Google Play processes the payment under its own terms. Your purchase is delivered once Google Play confirms the payment; if a payment is pending, it arrives when it clears. If you are under the age of majority where you live, ask a parent or guardian before buying. A pass or pack that lasts for a time (for example a weekly pack) gives its rewards for the time shown when you buy it.

Purchases are final, except as provided by Google Play’s refund policies and by your rights under consumer protection law, including Quebec’s Consumer Protection Act. You can ask for a refund through Google Play, or write to us. If a purchase goes through but it doesn’t arrive, write to support@fourcornersgames.ca with the order ID from your Google Play receipt and we will sort it out.

We may change prices or the packs on offer; this does not affect what you already have. Deleting your account removes your heroes, your items and your currency, including what you bought, without compensation.

The Android version will also offer optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. Rewards are given only for ads watched to the end, and the number of ad rewards per day may be limited. See the Privacy Policy for how ads work.
