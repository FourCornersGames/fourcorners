Delete your Shop Hand account

Shop Hand by Four Corners Games (Four Corners G.P.) · Last updated November 16, 2026

In short: you can delete your Shop Hand online account and everything saved online with it yourself, at any time, in the page. If you cannot open the page any more, email us and we will delete it for you.

Delete it in the page

  1. Open Shop Hand and tap About.
  2. Find the Online saving section.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data.
  4. Read the message. Tap Delete everything to confirm.
  5. If the page asks you to sign in again (this is for your safety), do so. For a Google account, sign in with Google again. For an email account, the page sends you a new sign-in link. Open it, and then delete.

Your online data is deleted straight away: your save and your account. The page also clears the data on this device. You cannot undo this. Save a backup file first if you want to keep your progress.

After deletion, the page does not create a new account unless you turn on online saving again.

Or ask us by email

If you cannot open the page any more, write to support@fourcornersgames.ca with the subject "Delete my Shop Hand account". Give us your user ID or your user name (shown in About), so we can find your account. We will delete it and confirm within 30 days. We may ask you a question only the account’s owner could answer before we delete it. If you used an email address to sign in, we may ask you to send the request from that address, because we do not copy it anywhere.

If you do not have the user ID or the user name, we may not be able to find your account. A guest account that no one uses is deleted automatically after 12 months with no save (24 months for a linked account). See the Privacy Policy.

What is deleted

  • Your save: droids, variants, fusion progress, rebirth numbers, Nova Shop choices and settings.
  • Your user name and your user ID record (the account type, the time of the last save).
  • Your sign-in account at Google Firebase Authentication, with the email address or Google link if you used one.
  • Your "trusted" record, if you had one.
  • Data on this device: your local save, the sign-in and the cached images.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backup files you saved on your own device. We cannot delete them. You can delete them yourself.
  • Emails you sent us, for as long as we need them to deal with your request.
  • Google may keep technical logs and backups for a short time under its own rules.
  • GitHub keeps the logs of visitors to the page under its own rules. We do not hold them.
  • A short note in our register that a deletion request was done (date only, with no user data).

Delete some data but keep your account

You can change your user name yourself. For other partial deletion (for example, only the save), write to us at support@fourcornersgames.ca with your user ID or user name and tell us what you want removed. We will do it and confirm within 30 days.

For everything else about your information, see our Privacy Policy.
