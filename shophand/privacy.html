Privacy Policy

Shop Hand · Last updated [DATE]

In short: Shop Hand collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no purchases. The app asks for no Android permission and makes no network connection, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. Your checklist stays on your device. If you back it up, it goes only where you choose to put it. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

This policy explains what Shop Hand does with information, and what you can do about it. We are a small studio in Quebec and we have kept it short and plain. If a later version of the app collects personal information, or connects to the internet, we will update this page before that version is released.

Who we are

Shop Hand is made by Four Corners Games (Four Corners G.P., Quatre Coins S.E.N.C. in French, a general partnership registered in Quebec), based in Quebec, Canada. Our Privacy Officer is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. You can reach our Privacy Officer at support@fourcornersgames.ca.

What the app is

Shop Hand is a free, unofficial, fan-made helper app for a Fortnite creator-made island. It helps you keep track of your own progress. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games, Disney, Lucasfilm or FOAD (the island's creator).

Who can use it

The app does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, whatever their age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

What we collect

Through the app: nothing about you. The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or any other information about you. It asks for no Android permission. It makes no network connection and sends nothing to us. It has no ads, no analytics, no purchases and no third-party software that collects data. It does not read your advertising ID, your location, your contacts, your photos or your files.

On your device, the app keeps one save file in its private storage. It holds only your own checklist: which items you have marked as owned, your progress numbers, and your filter and display settings. We cannot see this file. It is not included in Android's cloud backup, because we turned that backup off.

Backups you make. In About and settings you can copy your checklist as text, or save it to a file. You can also paste or load it back. When you save a file, Android's own file picker opens and you choose where the file goes. The app can only write to the place you choose and can only read the file you pick. We never receive these backups. If you put a backup in a cloud folder or send it to someone, that is your choice, and that service's own privacy rules apply.

Diagnostic log. The app keeps a local log on your device to help find faults. It records app events and technical details such as your device model, Android version and screen size. It does not record your name, account, location or contacts. The log stays on your device. It leaves your device only if you copy or save it and send it to us yourself. If you do, we receive what you send, together with your email address and message.

If you contact us. If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you write.

Why we collect it

  • to let the app work on your device (your checklist and settings);
  • to find and fix faults, when you choose to send us a diagnostic log;
  • to answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or to build a profile of you.

Who we share it with

The app shares nothing, because it collects nothing about you and has no connection to any server. Email you send us is handled by our email provider. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

The save file and the diagnostic log stay on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your Android settings, deletes them. Backup files you made are yours; the app cannot delete them, so delete them yourself when you no longer want them. We keep emails you send us, and any log you send, only as long as we need them to answer you and to improve the app.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, give you a copy, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to support@fourcornersgames.ca. We will reply within 30 days. If we cannot resolve a concern, you can contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

Security

We limit what we collect to what is needed: for the app itself, that is nothing. No system is perfectly secure. If an incident is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Commission d'accès à l'information as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Before a version of the app that uses personal information is released, we will publish the updated policy here and tell you in the app.
