Privacy Policy

Shop Hand · Last updated November 16, 2026

In short: Shop Hand is a free, unofficial fan-made tracker. It works on your device without an account. If you choose to turn on online saving, Shop Hand creates an anonymous guest account and saves your progress on Google’s servers in Montreal, Canada, so you can use it on other devices. We never ask for your real name. You can add a Google account or an email address to keep your account, but only Google keeps that email or name; we never copy it. There are no ads, no purchases, no analytics and no tracking. We never sell your data. You can delete your account and data yourself at any time in About.

This policy explains what personal information Shop Hand collects, why, and what you can do about it. We are a small studio in Quebec and we have tried to keep it short and plain.

Shop Hand is a test version. We will update this policy if what the page does changes.

Who we are

Shop Hand is made by Four Corners Games (Four Corners G.P., Quatre Coins S.E.N.C. in French, a general partnership registered in Quebec), based in Quebec, Canada. Our Privacy Officer is a partner of Four Corners Games, the person in charge of the protection of personal information. You can reach our Privacy Officer at support@fourcornersgames.ca.

Not official

Shop Hand is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games, Disney, Lucasfilm or FOAD (the creator of the island). It does not connect to Fortnite.

Who can use online saving

The page works for everyone without an account. Online saving is for people aged 14 and over. If you are under 14, a parent or guardian must agree before you turn it on. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 14 without that agreement. If you think a child under 14 has an online account without a parent’s agreement, write to us and we will delete it.

What we collect

If you do not turn on online saving. Your progress and settings stay in your browser on your device (browser storage). We do not receive them. Like any website, the page is delivered by GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address and browser information (see "Who we share it with").

If you turn on online saving. Shop Hand does these things:

  • It creates an anonymous guest account with Google Firebase Authentication. This gives your account a random ID (a user ID). Google’s sign-in service receives your IP address and device and browser information when it does this.
  • It gives you a random user name, for example "BrassGear42". You can change it (3 to 24 characters). Choose a name that is not your real name.
  • It saves your progress in a database (Google Cloud Firestore) in Montreal, Canada. The record holds: your user name, the type of sign-in ("guest", "google" or "email", nothing else about it), the time of the last save, a format number, and your progress: droids and variants you own, fusion progress, rebirth numbers, Nova Shop choices and your settings.
  • It keeps a copy on your device so the page works offline, and it keeps your sign-in on your device so you stay signed in (Firebase stores this in your browser’s IndexedDB).

If you link a Google account or an email address (optional).

  • Google: a Google pop-up opens. The page loads a script from Google (apis.google.com) to show it. Google may set its own cookies on its own websites. Shop Hand asks only for the sign-in. It does not ask for your name, your email or your photo.
  • Email: Firebase sends you a one-time sign-in link (no password). The email address is kept by Firebase Authentication to sign you in. While you use the link, the page keeps your email address in memory only.
  • We do not copy your email address, Google name or photo into your save, your browser storage or our tools. Our team tools do not show them. Honest note: Google’s Firebase console is the tool that holds your sign-in account. A project owner of that console can technically see the email address of an account there. We do not use it, and we will only look at it if we must (for example to answer a privacy request you send us).

What our team can see. Two partners of the studio can open a private team panel. It shows, for every online account: the user name, the user ID, the type of sign-in, the time of the last save and the saved progress. We can rename a user (for example to remove an offensive name) and we can mark a user as "trusted" (see below). We do not see emails, Google names or photos in this panel.

Trusted users and images. For a few people we know, the studio switches on access to some extra images that are stored in the database. If that is switched on for your account, we keep a record of your user ID, who switched it on and when. Every other visitor’s browser makes one request for the images, which is refused. The images that a trusted user receives are saved in the browser’s storage (IndexedDB) on that device. A marker that the request was refused is kept in your browser for 10 minutes.

Backups. You can save your progress to a file and load a file. The file stays on your device. We never receive it.

Cookies. The page does not set cookies. See our website privacy and cookies page.

Not collected. No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no location, no contacts, no photos. Your progress is not used to profile you, and the page does not take decisions about you.

Why we collect it

  • to save your progress and give it back to you on other devices (only if you turn on online saving);
  • to keep your account secure and to sign you in;
  • to look after the service: fix offensive user names, look at problems and respect your requests;
  • to answer you when you write to us.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for ads.

Who we share it with

We use two service providers:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Google sign-in). Your save is stored in a Google data centre in Montreal, Canada. The sign-in service (Firebase Authentication) may process sign-in data in the United States or elsewhere. Google acts for us as a service provider and uses the data under its own terms. Google also handles the Google pop-up if you choose it.
  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages), in the United States, delivers the page. As with any website, it receives your IP address and browser information. GitHub says it logs the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages sites for security. We do not receive those logs.

This means your information can be processed outside Quebec and outside Canada, in places with different privacy rules.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you clear your browser data, sign out or delete your account.
  • Online (user name, sign-in type, save, time of last save): until you delete your account, or until 12 months after your last save if you use a guest account, or 24 months after your last save if you linked a Google account or an email address. After that we delete the record and the account.
  • Sign-in data at Google (Firebase Authentication): until you delete your account or we delete it for inactivity.
  • Trusted record: until we switch it off or you delete your account.
  • Emails you send us: as long as we need them to answer you.
  • Backups at Google: We keep no backups of our own. Google may keep technical backups for a short time under its own rules.
  • GitHub logs: under GitHub’s own rules.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask for a copy in a usual format, ask us to delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. You can do most of this yourself:

  • Correct: change your user name in the page.
  • Copy: use the backup button in About to save your progress to a file.
  • Delete and withdraw consent: in About, choose "Delete my account and data". Or see Delete your account.

For anything else, write to support@fourcornersgames.ca. We will reply within 30 days. If we cannot settle a concern, you can write to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

Because your account is anonymous, we may ask you for your user ID (shown in About) and a way to prove it is yours, so that we do not give your information to someone else.

Security

Your save is protected by sign-in and by database rules that only let your account read and write your own save. Only our two partners can open the team panel. Everything travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure. We keep a register of confidentiality incidents. If an incident could cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Commission d’accès à l’information, as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will change the date above. For important changes, we will tell you in the page before they apply.
