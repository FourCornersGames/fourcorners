About the studio

We’re two friends making the kind of games we like to play: quick to pick up, full of personality, and fair to the people who play them.

The studio is named after the Four Corners, the intersection between our childhood homes.

Every game we make ships in English and French. We keep what we collect to what a game actually needs, and we explain it in plain language.
