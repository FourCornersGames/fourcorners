Refund Policy

YarnToss, Whiskers Cafe and Pocket Quest · Last updated November 9, 2026

In short: everything you buy in our games is paid through Google Play; we never charge you directly. You can ask Google Play for a refund, or write to us and we will look at it. If something you paid for didn’t arrive, or you were charged twice, we will deliver it or refund it. Your rights under consumer protection law always apply.

This policy covers purchases made inside our games on Android: YarnToss, Whiskers Cafe and Pocket Quest. Our games are still in development, and some don’t sell anything yet. This website doesn’t sell anything. Each game’s Terms of Service (YarnToss, Whiskers Cafe, Pocket Quest) has the full rules for purchases; if this page and a game’s terms ever differ, the terms apply.

How purchases work

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Google charges you, sends you the receipt, and shares the order details with us (see each game’s privacy policy). We never see your card or bank details. The price, in your currency, is shown before you pay.

How to ask for a refund

  • Through Google Play: Google can refund many purchases itself, depending on what you bought, when, and where you live. See Google Play’s refund policies.
  • Through us: write to support@fourcornersgames.ca with the game, the order ID from your Google Play receipt (it starts with “GPA.”), and what went wrong. We can refund a Google Play order ourselves.

When we will refund

We will deliver what you paid for, or refund it, if:

  • you were charged but the item or currency never arrived in the game;
  • you were charged more than once for the same purchase;
  • what you bought doesn’t work as described because of a problem on our side.

Otherwise, purchases are final, except as provided by Google Play’s refund policies and by your rights under consumer protection law, including Quebec’s Consumer Protection Act. Nothing on this page limits a right you have under the law.

Things to know

  • Subscriptions and passes. If something is sold as a subscription that renews by itself, you can cancel it at any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). You keep its benefits until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting your account or the game does not cancel a subscription.
  • Purchases made without permission. If someone, such as a child, bought something on your Google account without your permission, ask Google Play for a refund, and turn on purchase authentication in the Google Play settings so it doesn’t happen again.
  • Refunded purchases. When an order is refunded or reversed, we may remove what it gave you from your account.
  • Deleting your account. Deleting your account removes what you bought, without a refund (see each game’s terms).
  • Ads. Rewards for watching an optional ad are free, so there is nothing to refund.
  • In-game currency has no cash value and can’t be exchanged for money.
